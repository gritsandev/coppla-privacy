# Delete your Coppla account

App name: **Coppla**
Developer: Dmitrii Gritsan

You can delete your account and all data associated with it at any time.
There are two ways to do it.

---

## Option 1 — inside the app

1. Open Coppla.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

Deletion happens immediately. Nothing is kept in a recycle bin and the
action cannot be undone.

---

## Option 2 — by email

If you have already uninstalled the app, or cannot sign in, send a message
to **gritsan.dev@gmail.com** from the email address your account is
registered with, with the subject line **Delete my Coppla account**.

We use the sender address to confirm ownership of the account, so please
write from that address. We will delete the account and reply to confirm
within 30 days, usually much sooner.

---

## What gets deleted

Everything tied to your account is removed permanently:

- your email address and password
- your display name, chosen colour and language
- your membership in a couple, and the couple itself if no one else remains in it
- all savings challenges you created: settings, goals and amounts
- every cell you marked, including the amounts recorded with them
- the device tokens used to deliver notifications to you

Your partner's own account is not affected. Data that belongs to both of
you — the shared challenge and its history — is removed together with the
couple once it is left empty.

## What is not affected

Anonymous usage statistics (which screens were opened, whether a game was
created or completed) are not linked to your account or to your identity,
and cannot be traced back to you after deletion. They contain no amounts
and no personal information. Because there is nothing in them that
identifies you, they are unaffected by account deletion.
