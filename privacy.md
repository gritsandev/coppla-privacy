# Privacy Policy for Coppla

**Last updated: August 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how Coppla ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses,
and protects your information when you use our mobile application (the "Service").

---

## 1. Information We Collect

To provide you with the core functionality of our Service, we collect the
following data:

**Account Information.** When you log in via Google, we collect your email
address and basic profile information (such as your name) provided by the
authentication service.

**Usage Data.** We store the state of your in-app progress (such as which bingo
cells have been marked) and a unique identifier that links your account to your
partner's account to enable real-time synchronization.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data exclusively to:

- Create and manage your account.
- Synchronize the app's state between you and your linked partner.
- Provide customer support and respond to your requests.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.
We use trusted third-party services to operate our app:

**Supabase.** We use Supabase as our backend provider for secure database
storage and authentication. All data is securely transmitted and stored on
their servers.

---

## 4. Data Deletion and Account Removal

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated
data at any time.

You can delete your account directly within the app by navigating to
**Settings → Delete Account**. Alternatively, you can request data deletion by
contacting us at the email address provided below.

Upon request, all your account information and associated progress data will be
permanently removed from our databases.
